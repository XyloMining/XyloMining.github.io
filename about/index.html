About

Bitcoin mining, in the open

I'm Xylo, and I'm based in Australia. I mine Bitcoin in two ways: through GoMining, where I own digital hashrate that earns daily Bitcoin rewards, and with my own GPU through Kryptex.

I document it as I go: the setup, the costs, what the numbers really look like, and what I get wrong. I make short videos for YouTube, TikTok and Instagram, post on X and Reddit, and write longer pieces on Medium.

What this site is for

Mining content online is full of hype. This site is the opposite: one place where you can learn how mining works, test the maths yourself, and see my real numbers, including the unexciting ones. It is education and a record of my own experience, not advice to buy anything.

  • Guides – Plain-English explainers on hashrate, difficulty, maintenance fees and how to work out profitability, plus a GoMining quickstart.
  • Calculator – Put in your own numbers and stress test them before you spend anything.
  • My results – What my own mining has actually done, shown as my numbers and not typical ones.
  • Stats – Views, likes and followers across my channels, updated automatically.
  • Socials – My latest videos and posts from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X and Reddit in one place.
  • Articles – Longer write-ups on Medium.
  • Referrals – The tools I use, with my referral codes.

How I keep it honest

  • No promises of profit. Returns depend on Bitcoin's price, difficulty and fees, and can be negative.
  • When I share my own results, they are my numbers, not typical, and yours can be lower.
  • Claims in my guides link to their sources.
  • Some videos use AI-generated narration and are labelled as such.
